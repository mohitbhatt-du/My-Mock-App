Hey guys, why does Dijkstra algorithm fail on graphs with negative edge weights even if there are no negative cycles?
🥝
Cosmic Kiwi #312 11:17 AM
Because Dijkstra follows a Greedy approach: once a vertex is marked visited and added to the finalized set, it assumes its shortest distance is permanent and never relaxes its edges again. A negative edge later can invalidate that assumption!
🍈
Radiant Guava #518 11:19 AM
Exactly! Bellman-Ford on the other hand relaxes all edges |V| - 1 times, which is why it can handle negative edges in O(VE) time. Very common direct MCQ!
